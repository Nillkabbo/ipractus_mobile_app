## What We're Building

A comprehensive mobile sports platform that enables athletes, coaches, and fans to:

- Connect and share updates through a social feed
- Communicate via real-time chat and video calls
- Manage teams and team activities
- Stream live content
- Organize and share media libraries

---

## Features Breakdown

### Week 1: Foundation (Foundation & Authentication)
- User registration and login (Athlete/Coach/Fan roles)
- Secure authentication with persistent sessions
- User profile management with avatar upload
- Core navigation structure

### Week 2: Social & Communication (Social Feed & Group Chat)
- Social feed with text, images, and video playback
- Create and share posts
- Group chat creation and management
- Real-time messaging with push notifications
- Share images in conversations

### Week 3: Teams & Real-Time (Teams, Video Calls & Live Streaming)
- Browse and join teams
- Team-specific posts and announcements
- 1-on-1 video and audio calls
- Live streaming capability (go live, watch streams)
- Real-time notifications

### Week 4: Media & Launch (Media Library & Polish)
- Personal media library (photos, videos, audio)
- Album organization
- Upload and share media
- Performance optimization
- iOS TestFlight and Android APK delivery

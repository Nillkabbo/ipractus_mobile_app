Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2023

Welcome to iPractus. Your privacy is critical to us. This policy outlines how we handle your personal information when you use our sports community platform to connect with teammates and coaches.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as your profile information, sports interests, performance metrics, and communication preferences. We also collect certain information automatically when you use our services, including device details and app usage patterns.

How We Use Your Data

Your data helps us personalize your experience. We use it to suggest local sports groups, facilitate matchmaking for matches, and improve the technical stability of the iPractus app. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Third-Party Sharing

We may share information with service providers who perform functions on our behalf, such as cloud hosting and analytics. These partners are strictly bound by confidentiality agreements.
